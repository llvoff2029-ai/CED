01 / WHO WE ARE

Part theatrical design shop, part engineering lab.

CED sits inside Pingry's Theatrical Design & Technology program. We take on original, long-term, multidisciplinary builds — the kind usually reserved for professional Imagineering studios — and submit them to design competitions including Walt Disney Imaginations.

STORY

Concept & theme

Every build starts as narrative — a world, a character, a guest journey — before it becomes a spec sheet. We research, pitch, and iterate concepts the way a studio would.

SYSTEM

Engineering & show control

Animatronics, ride mechanisms, and show systems are designed and documented to production standard — motion, control logic, and safety, not just looks.

CRAFT

Fabrication

We cut, wire, paint, and program everything ourselves — where math, science, and the humanities meet on the shop floor to produce something extraordinary.

02 / PAST PROJECT

Meet Jojo.

Jojo is a fully articulated animatronic macaw, built entirely by CED students. He moves and "speaks" using the same character-animation principles used to bring 3D characters to life on film and TV — translated into servos, linkages, and custom show control.

BUILT BY
Pingry Theatrical Design & Technology students
DISCIPLINE
Animatronic animation & show control
METHOD
Keyframed character animation, adapted to physical mechanism
BEHAVIOR
Original movement & interaction sequences

04 / HOW WE WORK

From pitch to working mechanism.

Every CED project — from a single animatronic to a full park — moves through the same four phases.

01

Concept & pitch

Research, theming, and story development, pitched and critiqued as a team.

02

Design & documentation

Drawings, models, and engineering specs — the plans a fabrication team can actually build from.

03

Build & program

Fabrication, wiring, and show control programming, done in-house by students.

04

Test & present

Refinement, demonstration, and submission — including to competitions like Walt Disney Imaginations.

Want to help build the next Jojo — or the ride?

CED is open to students interested in design, engineering, art, or fabrication — no prior experience required. If you can sketch it, wire it, or figure out how to move it, there's a place for you here.
